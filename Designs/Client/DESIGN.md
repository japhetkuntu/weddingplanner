## Brand & Style

The Ovutor Wedding Planning Platform aims to provide a calm, warm, editorial, and understated user experience across all three of its surfaces (Admin Portal, Client Portal, Public Wedding Website). The design should prioritize consistency across these surfaces, use familiar UI patterns, and reduce anxiety and cognitive load for its specific users. The Client Portal and Public Site should lean towards a warmer, more editorial feel with generous whitespace and real photography, aligning with the ovutor.com marketing site.
